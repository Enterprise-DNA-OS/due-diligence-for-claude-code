# DealRoom target research

Checked 12 October 2026. This is the M&A due-diligence family, not engineering change control (OpenBOM), stakeholder engagement (Jambo) or the existing private-capital sourcing CRM.

## Scores and annual bill

US$20,000/year is the starting DealRoom price still displayed on the [related FirmRoom vendor pricing surface](https://landing-prod.firmroom.com/pricing). The [current DealRoom pricing page](https://dealroom.net/products/pricing) publishes no amount and prices by deal volume under an annual commitment. This is a vendor starting-price indicator, not a verified NZ or AU customer invoice or a current quote. Our public comparison uses no dollar figure.

These are editorial judgments. FirmRoom centres on secure file sharing, a poor fit for a records-only build. Yamdu's published starting plans do not establish a five-figure base bill. DealRoom supports the agency acquisition strategy in the Command Centre. Local customer markdown search did not establish a DealRoom buyer. No live CRM read or customer invoice was verified; the buyer score stays low. Alternative search listings exist but keyword volume was not measured. The selection does not claim proved local demand.

## Product and export sources

- [Current pricing](https://dealroom.net/products/pricing): deal-volume pricing, unlimited users, annual commitment, no per-document billing, diligence, integration and reporting. Do not claim DealRoom charges per seat or lacks reporting.
- [Vendor help guide](https://dealroom.net/resources/help-guides/dealroom-help-guides): requests, assignees and reviewers, request status reports, Excel export, separate document permissions. The guide does not publish a guaranteed export header schema. The importer accepts a request-level CSV saved from Excel with explicit column mapping.
- [Vendor API](https://dealroom.net/api): further room, request and document operations exist; this build does not require API credentials.
- [FirmRoom pricing](https://firmroom.com/pricing): compared as a neighbouring document-room product, not selected as the diligence workflow target.
- [Yamdu pricing evidence](https://support.yamdu.com/en/articles/6588-activating-student-pricing): lower-end production tool candidate; no verified expensive buyer bill established.

The ten questions are verified queries, not exclusive claims. DealRoom offers configurable reporting and can answer overlapping questions. The free version rebuilds request review, risk follow-up and handover records, not the vendor's certified storage and collaboration service. Fictional demo outputs are captured from actual commands.
